# Local SEO for a Dog Boarding / Daycare / Grooming Business

Most people searching for boarding, daycare, or grooming are looking for a provider **near them** — so local SEO, not generic SEO, is where the leverage is. The goal is to rank in the Google Local Pack (top-3 map results) and in the organic results for "near me" and city-qualified queries. Google Business Profile is one input; the items below are the website + off-site work that supports it.

## NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone)

- Your **Name, Address, and Phone must be byte-for-byte identical** everywhere — website, Google Business Profile, Yelp, Nextdoor, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Facebook, and every directory.
- Inconsistent NAP (e.g., "Suite 5" vs "Ste. 5," two different phone numbers) **dilutes ranking signals** and can trigger penalties. Pick one canonical format and enforce it.

## Local citations

- Build listings on the major aggregators and pet-specific directories. Quality + consistency matter more than raw quantity.
- Audit citations **quarterly** for drift (old addresses, closed-duplicate listings, wrong phone). Duplicates especially hurt.

## Service-area landing pages

- Create a **dedicated page per service AND per town/neighborhood** you serve — e.g., `/dog-boarding-[city]`, `/doggy-daycare-[city]`, `/dog-grooming-[city]`.
- Each page should have genuinely unique content (local landmarks, directions, that town's specifics) — not a template with the city name swapped. Thin, duplicated doorway pages get filtered.
- Target natural query patterns: "dog boarding [city]," "doggy daycare near [neighborhood]," "[city] dog groomer."

## LocalBusiness schema markup

- Add **`LocalBusiness` structured data** (JSON-LD) to the homepage and location pages. Include:
  - `name`, full `address` (street + city + region + postal code), `telephone`
  - `geo` coordinates, `openingHoursSpecification`
  - `url`, `image`, `priceRange`, and `sameAs` links to your social/directory profiles
- Schema helps Google confirm your NAP and can enable richer results. Keep the schema values identical to your visible NAP.

## Review velocity

- **A steady stream of recent reviews matters more than total count.** Ten reviews this quarter beats fifty reviews from three years ago.
- Engineer velocity into operations: automated text/email review request at pickup, a short link, and staff trained to ask happy clients.
- Spread requests across platforms (Google primary, then Yelp/Facebook) but keep Google the focus for the Local Pack.

## On-page & technical fundamentals

- **Mobile-friendly** (most pet searches are mobile) with a fast load — target Largest Contentful Paint under ~2.5s.
- A frictionless **online booking funnel** — every extra step loses bookings.
- Clear H1/headers using the "service in city" pattern near the top of location pages.
- Internal links between service pages, location pages, and the blog.

## Local link building

- Local backlinks build "prominence": vet clinics, dog trainers, breeders, pet-supply stores, local pet-event sponsorships, chamber of commerce, local news/round-ups.

## Recommended cadence

Run a **quarterly local-SEO audit** covering: citation consistency, review velocity & rating, location-page on-page health, new local backlinks, and competitor movement in the Local Pack.
