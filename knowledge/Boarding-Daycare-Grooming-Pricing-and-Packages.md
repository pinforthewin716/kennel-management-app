# Boarding, Daycare & Grooming — Pricing and Packages Benchmark

Benchmark rates and package structures for a US dog boarding/daycare/grooming/training facility, with a Chicago-suburb (Lake County, IL) lens for Happy Pup Manor. Use this to build the rate card. All figures are 2026 market benchmarks; **regional variation is large** — rural < suburban < urban < luxury-urban. Happy Pup Manor's North-Shore-Chicago / Lake County position supports rates at the upper-suburban-to-luxury end, especially given the doodle/designer-breed clientele and 30-acre property.

---

## 1. Boarding (per night)

| Tier | National range | Chicago metro |
|---|---|---|
| Budget / standard kennel | $35–$55 | $50–$65 |
| Mid / suite | $55–$85 | $65–$85 |
| Luxury / private suite (gourmet meals, pool, daily grooming) | $85–$150+ | $85–$150+; some start at $125/24 hrs |

- National standard band is **$35–$85/night**; Chicago owners typically budget **$50–$85**, luxury **$75–$150+**.
- PetSmart PetsHotel reference points (2026): ~$37/night standard suite, ~$52/night premium suite — useful as the "chain floor" you price above.
- **Multi-night / second-dog discounts** are standard: e.g. 10–15% off nights 7+, or a reduced per-night rate for a second dog sharing a suite.
- **Holiday/peak surcharge:** +20–40% over a stay is common via holiday premiums, exit baths, and late-pickup fees.

**Suggested HPM positioning:** anchor standard boarding around **$70–$85/night**, with a luxury/suite tier at **$95–$125+**, given Lake County affluence and designer-breed niche.

---

## 2. Daycare

| Structure | National | Chicago metro |
|---|---|---|
| Half day | $20–$25 | ~$25–$30 |
| Full day (drop-in) | $30–$50 (avg ~$40) | $30–$55 |

**Multi-day & monthly packages (the profit engine — drives weekday fill + cash-flow stability):**
- **Punch-card / day packs:** 5-, 10-, 20-day packs at **5–15% off** the drop-in rate. Bigger pack = bigger discount = more prepaid commitment.
- **Monthly tiered memberships:** typical tiers of **8, 12, or 20 visits/month**, often with limited rollover to protect cash flow.
- **Unlimited monthly membership:** 7-day access, free early drop-off / late pickup, and frequently **50% off boarding** as the headline perk — no per-visit booking needed. This is the premium anchor tier.

**Why memberships over drop-in:** prepaid, predictable revenue; locks in weekday attendance (the hardest slots to fill); raises customer lifetime value. Recommended default model: drop-in rate + 3 day-pack sizes + 2–3 monthly membership tiers + 1 unlimited.

---

## 3. Grooming

- National grooming: **$40–$75** typical; simple services (nail trim, bath) start ~$15, full grooms $50+ and scale with size/coat/breed.
- **Breed/size/coat-driven:** doodles and long-coat designer breeds (HPM's niche) sit at the **high end** — full doodle grooms commonly $75–$120+ in metro markets. Price by coat type, not just weight.
- **Bundle lever:** attach grooming/nail trims to boarding checkout ("exit bath/groom") or to daycare days — raises ticket size with **zero new customer-acquisition cost**. Exit-bath-before-pickup is one of the highest-margin add-ons.

---

## 4. Add-Ons & Upsells (raise average ticket)

Facilities report **+$15–$25 per reservation** from add-ons. Common menu:
- **Enrichment blocks** — structured play, agility, sniff/puzzle, "nap & calm" time — sold as paid upgrades per day.
- **One-on-one play / extra walks** (especially valuable for boarding).
- **Training add-ons** — board-and-train, single private sessions, "manners tune-up" during a boarding stay (a natural fit given HPM is already a training facility — this is a differentiator most kennels can't offer).
- **Grooming/bath add-ons** (see above).
- **Premium suites / webcam access / report cards & photos.**
- **Late pickup / early drop-off / holiday surcharges** (operational fees that also protect schedule).
- **Treats, photos, "pawsitive" extras** at checkout.

---

## 5. Common Package / Membership Models (pick a mix)

1. **À la carte** — pure per-night / per-day pricing. Simplest; lowest commitment; weakest cash flow.
2. **Prepaid day packs** — 5/10/20 days at tiered discounts. Easy to sell, no recurring-billing system needed.
3. **Tiered monthly memberships** — 8/12/20 visits/month at set price; protects recurring revenue.
4. **Unlimited membership** — flat monthly, all-access, bundles boarding discount; premium anchor that makes other tiers look reasonable.
5. **Bundled "stay" packages** — boarding + exit groom + enrichment sold as one SKU at a slight discount vs. à la carte (raises attach rate).
6. **Puppy / new-client intro packages** — discounted first pack to convert trial users into members; pairs naturally with HPM's puppy-training side.

**Recommended HPM rate-card skeleton:**
- Boarding: standard $70–85, luxury suite $95–125+, multi-night & 2nd-dog discounts, holiday surcharge.
- Daycare: full $40–50 / half $25–30; 10- & 20-day packs (10–15% off); 12-visit and unlimited monthly memberships (unlimited bundles 50% off boarding).
- Grooming: priced by coat (doodle full groom $75–120+); exit-bath add-on.
- Add-ons: enrichment $15–25/day, private training session, extra play, late-pickup fee.
- Lean into the **training differentiator** — board-and-train and in-stay manners sessions are upsells competitors in the area likely can't match.
