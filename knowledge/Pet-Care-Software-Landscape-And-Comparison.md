# Pet-Care Booking/Management Software: Landscape & Feature Comparison

Reference for evaluating — and ultimately **replacing** — Gingr + Zoho with custom software at Happy Pup Manor. Pricing below is approximate and changes often; **verify live before quoting**.

## The incumbents being replaced

- **Gingr** — the category leader for boarding/daycare. Feature set: pet-parent **mobile app + portal**, **online booking**, automated reminders (text/email), **pet report cards**, marketing campaigns, **staff scheduling**, multi-location, **in-depth reporting**, and **QuickBooks integration**. This is the functional bar a custom build must meet or beat.
- **Zoho** — general-purpose CRM/business suite (not pet-specific). Used here for the CRM/billing/automation layer Gingr doesn't fully cover.

Typical market pricing runs **~$45 to $150+/month** depending on tier and add-ons.

## The feature set a Gingr+Zoho replacement MUST cover

Group these as the build's module checklist:

**1. Booking / reservations**
- Online self-service booking (boarding, daycare, grooming, training)
- Real-time **capacity/kennel availability** by run/suite/size group
- Recurring/package bookings, waitlists, holiday-blackout rules
- Drop-off / pickup scheduling and reminders

**2. Billing & payments**
- Integrated card processing, deposits, packages/credits, tips
- Recurring billing, invoicing, customer balances/account credit
- **QuickBooks (or equivalent accounting) sync** — table stakes; Gingr has it

**3. Vaccination tracking**
- Store vaccine records with **expiration dates**
- **Auto-flag/block check-in on expired records** before every visit
- Reminder notifications to owners ahead of expiry

**4. Customer portal + pet-parent app**
- Self-service profile/pet management, document upload (vax records)
- Booking, payment, and **digital report cards / photos** (a top retention driver)
- Two-way messaging / notifications

**5. Operations**
- **Staff scheduling**, run/suite assignment, feeding & medication schedules
- Group assignments by size/temperament, incident reporting
- Belongings/inventory tracking per stay

**6. Reporting & marketing**
- Financial KPIs (revenue, occupancy %, average stay value)
- Occupancy/utilization, no-show/cancellation, customer LTV
- Automated marketing (review requests, win-back, seasonal campaigns), CRM (this is where **Zoho** currently fills the gap)

**7. Multi-service / multi-location** (boarding + daycare + grooming + training under one roof)

## Build-vs-buy notes for the custom path

- The **vaccination expiry hard-block** and **digital report cards** are the two features owners cite as highest-value and worth getting right early.
- A **branded pet-parent mobile app/portal** (RunLoyal/Gingr both lead here) is a key differentiator and retention lever.
- Replacing Zoho means the custom build must own **CRM + automated marketing**, not just operations — easy to under-scope.
