# Google Business Profile Optimization for Pet Boarding & Daycare

The Google Business Profile (GBP) is the single highest-leverage local marketing asset for a boarding/daycare facility. It controls whether you appear in the Google Maps "Local Pack" (the top-3 map results) when a pet parent searches "dog boarding near me." Below is a practical, facility-specific optimization playbook.

## Categories

- **Primary category** is the strongest single category ranking signal. Choose the most specific match — `Pet boarding service` for boarding, or `Dog day care center` if daycare is the lead service. Do not use a generic catch-all like "Pet store."
- **Add secondary categories** for every real service you offer: `Dog day care center`, `Pet groomer`, `Dog trainer`, `Pet sitter`. Each unlocks ranking for that service's searches.
- Don't keyword-stuff categories with services you don't actually provide — Google can suspend the profile.

## Business name

- Use your **real, legal/branded name exactly as it appears on signage** (e.g., "Happy Pup Manor"). Adding keywords you don't have on the building ("Happy Pup Manor Dog Boarding & Grooming") is against Google guidelines and risks suspension, though a genuinely-branded descriptor is lower-risk. Keep it consistent with your website and citations.

## Photos

- Profiles with **recent, regularly added photos get materially more views and clicks** than profiles with a stale handful.
- Lead with a clean, professional **exterior shot** (trust + curb recognition), then interiors: play yards, boarding suites, grooming area, happy dogs, staff.
- Add new photos on a steady cadence (e.g., weekly). Geotag/caption where possible. Encourage customers to add photos too — user photos are a freshness signal.

## Google Posts

- Publish **Posts roughly weekly** — they are a freshness/engagement signal and surface in the profile. Use them for holiday-boarding availability, daycare openings, grooming specials, events, new staff, and seasonal reminders (holiday booking deadlines).

## Reviews & local pack ranking

- The Local Pack heavily weights **review volume AND recency**. A facility with **50+ reviews and steady monthly additions outranks** an older business with a stagnant count.
- Build a **review-request workflow** at checkout (text/email link the moment a happy client picks up). Aim for a continuous trickle, not a one-time burst.
- **Respond to every review within ~48 hours**, especially negatives — responses are a ranking/engagement signal and shape buyer trust.
- Conversion lift is real: facilities with 50+ reviews and a 4.7+ rating have been reported to convert Maps impressions into calls at roughly 2.9x the rate of facilities with under 20 reviews.

## The three pillars of local pack ranking (Google's documented model)

1. **Relevance** — categories, services, and profile description match the search.
2. **Distance** — proximity of the facility to the searcher (you can't change location, but accurate address + service area help).
3. **Prominence** — review signals, citations, links, and overall web presence.

## Profile completeness checklist

- Accurate hours (including holiday hours — critical for boarding).
- Services + service descriptions filled out.
- Booking/website link and a working phone number.
- Q&A section seeded with real questions (vaccination requirements, drop-off hours, what to bring).
- Attributes set (e.g., "online appointments," "identifies as women-owned" if applicable).
